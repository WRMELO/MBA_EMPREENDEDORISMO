INTRODUCTION
As a worldwide supplier of fluidized bed combustion plants the reference list of Austrian Energy & Envi-
ronment comprises 82 steam generators with capacities from 9 t/h to 400 t/h with a fuel range from coal,
biomass, sludge, residue derived fuel (RDF) to various industrial wastes. Depending on special requirements
of the various fuels and the size of the boiler different combustion systems like the bubbling fluidized bed or
the external circulating fluidized bed technology will be used. All of these systems have been developed by
AE&E and will allow maximum flexibility in the range of fuels. Beside environmentally sound combustion
with low emissions, maximum boiler efficiency and availability is the main target.
In this paper two new biomass fired plants, one located in Austria and one in Sweden will be described and
operation results reported.
COMBUSTION TECHNOLOGY
The applied combustion technology in these plants is the ECOFLUID® bubbling fluidised bed technology.
The main feature of this technology is the principle of staged combustion of the fuel.
The oxygen level in the fluidised bed is limited and hence only a part of the fuel is combusted, whereas the
rest of the fuel is gasified. This can be achieved by adding a substoichiometric amount of oxygen (lambda
approx. 0.35) to the fuel. However, in order to maintain a constant lambda and hence temperature in the bed,
this would result in the necessity to vary the fluidisation gas volume flow. Therefore the fluidisation of the
bed would be dependent on the heating value of the fuel. Since this effect is not desired, primary air is mixed
with recirculated flue gas. This allows the control of lambda 1.0

BIOMASS FIRED POWER PLANT TIMELKAM/AUSTRIA
The technical concept comprises a biomass combined heat and power plant (CHP) with a new bubbling flu-
idized bed boiler. For electricity production an existing steam turbine is used. With an estimated operation of
8000 h/year 95 GWh of electricity and 88 GWh of district heat can be produced, which corresponds to a
supply of 26.000 and 5.800 private households with electricity and heat, respectively.
As fuel wood chips and wood residues forest industry as well as from wood and saw mill industry (bark, saw
dust, grinding dust) and waste wood is used. After a successful test of firing meat and bone residues last
year, a permanent installation for feeding this fuel is currently installed. The permit was granted in 2002, the
erection was started at the end of 2004 and the boiler reached the PAC in February 2006. Green power feed
in tariffs were granted for ten years according to the Federal Green Power Regulation.
Basis for the design of the fluidized bed combustion
Considering the fuel properties and the range of the heating values, the decision was made to use an
ECOFLUID® bubbling bed with substoichiometric combustion in the bed and a secondary air supply for the
post combustion chamber. The design values of the plant can be found in Table 1.
The plant is approved according to the § 29 Abs. 1 Z3 Abfallwirtschaftsgesetz (AWG), which sets emission

Together with the wood wastes incombustible parts like stones, nails, screws and metals are usually fed to
the fluidized bed, where they collect and hinder the fluidization. To prevent this, an open type air distributor
in AE&E –design was built. This air distributor enables the drain of bed material over the whole bed cross

culated flue gas is used, too.

Bed Material and Ash Handling
The bed material together with the impurities is drained continuously from the furnace hoppers with vibrat-
ing conveyors. The coarse parts are separated from the fine particles and sand in an air classifier. The fine

material from the classifier air is separated by a separator and can be either returned into the bed reducing the
sand consumption of the boiler or can be sent to the ash silo for disposal.

than guaranteed.

BIOMASS FIRED POWER PLANT SÖDERHAMN/SWEDEN
The Söderhamn boiler consists of a new fluidised bed boiler and a new back-pressure steam turbine for elec-
tricity and heat production. After the flue gas cleaning a flue gas condensation unit is installed to obtain addi-
tional low temperature district heat. Hence, an efficiency of the overall system of more than 100% based on
the LHV can be obtained. Wood chips and wood residues from the forest industry are used as fuel. The erec-
tion was started in August 2006; the commissioning of the plant was concluded in November 2007.

Denoxing of the Flue Gases
In this plant the secondary air nozzles are installed right above the secondary air, to increase the residence
time of the ammonia and to ensure also proper Denoxing of the fuel gases even when the plant operates in
part load. The location of these nozzles was found by the comprehensive simulation works done by TU-
Wien, which are also part of these proceedings. As SNCR-media ammonia is used.
Operation Results
The commissioning of the plant started in June 2007. The PAC could be signed at the end of December
2007. In January the plant could meet all guaranteed data at the performance test. The plant produced right
away the desired high steam data of 503°C within the load range of 100-75%. The investigations of the
SNCR system by a CFD simulation proofed to be a very good approach since the results could be seen also
clearly during real operation. The emission limits could be fulfilled in all load cases. Further results will be
presented during the conference since the results are currently processed.
CONCLUSION
Based on these results it can be seen that the ECOFLUID® technology is capable to combust a broad range of
different biogenic fuels. The range includes “conventional” fuels like wood, wood chips or bark but also fu-
els like chicken litter or bio sludge. Furthermore, it is possible to keep the strict emission limits of the Fed-
eral German Emissions Control Ordinance at the same time. By using the sub stoichiometric combustion
technology which enables high live steam parameters biomass fired power plants are able to obtain high effi-
ciencies. Currently a new biomass plant in Umea/Sweden is engineered with live steam parameters of 543°C
and 145 bar . This is a major contribution to enhance green power production on an environmentally friendly
